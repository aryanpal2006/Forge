# ICAR Agronomic Practice Guide: Soybean (Glycine max)

## RULE-SOY-DROUGHT-01: Soil Moisture Deficit & Dry Spell Mitigation
- **Condition**: Rainfall deficit > 30% for 14 consecutive days during flowering or pod filling (R3-R5 stages).
- **Action**: Apply straw or crop residue mulching at 5 tonnes/ha to suppress evaporative loss. Spray anti-transpirant 1% Potassium Nitrate (KNO3) or Salicylic acid (100 ppm) to conserve internal water potential.
- **Irrigation**: If borewell/well water is available, apply life-saving micro-irrigation (sprinkler preferred) at critical pod-development stage.

## RULE-SOY-PEST-02: Spodoptera & Girdle Beetle Outbreak Control
- **Condition**: Temperature 28°C–34°C with intermittent cloudy spells and high humidity (>75%).
- **Action**: Erect 10-12 bird perches per acre and install 4-5 pheromone traps. On crossing Economic Threshold Level (ETL of 2 larvae/meter row), spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L.

## RULE-SOY-MARKET-03: Selling Signal Evaluation
- **Condition**: Mandi modal price rising above 15-day moving average with high crush demand.
- **Action**: Retain high-grade cleaned soybean in moisture-proof bags (<10% seed moisture) for gradual staggered sales over 3-4 weeks rather than distress selling at harvest peak.
