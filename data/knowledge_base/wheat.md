# ICAR Agronomic Practice Guide: Wheat (Triticum aestivum)

## RULE-WHEAT-TERMINAL-HEAT-01: Terminal Heat Stress Defense
- **Condition**: Temperature > 30°C during grain filling stage (February-March).
- **Action**: Terminal heat forces premature senescence and shriveled grains. Spray 0.5% Potassium Chloride or 0.2% Alpha-tocopherol at heading and milk stages. Maintain light surface moisture via sprinkler irrigation to lower ambient crop temperature by 2°C–3°C.

## RULE-WHEAT-RUST-02: Yellow & Brown Rust Warning
- **Condition**: Cool nights (10°C–15°C) followed by morning dew and cloudy days.
- **Action**: Check leaf blades for yellow stripe uredinia. Spray Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 ml/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/L at first appearance.

## RULE-WHEAT-CRI-03: Crown Root Initiation Irrigation
- **Condition**: 20-25 days after sowing (DAS).
- **Action**: The CRI stage is the single most critical moisture-sensitive growth window. Ensure first irrigation is applied within this window to guarantee adequate tillering and root anchoring.
