# ICAR Agronomic Practice Guide: Potato (Solanum tuberosum)

## RULE-POTATO-BLIGHT-01: Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans) Epidemic Prevention
- **Condition**: Continuous relative humidity > 85%, temperature 12°C–22°C with cloudiness/drizzle for 48 hours.
- **Action**: High epidemic hazard. Apply prophylactic spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L. If initial water-soaked spots appear on leaf tips, switch to systemic fungicide Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 3 g/L.

## RULE-POTATO-WATERLOGGING-02: Furrow Drainage & Tuber Rot Prevention
- **Condition**: Rainfall > 40 mm in Gangetic plain alluvial silts during tuberization.
- **Action**: Deepen intra-ridge drainage channels to evacuate standing water within 6 hours. Standing water induces lenticel enlargement and bacterial soft rot (*Pectobacterium carotovorum*).

## RULE-POTATO-HARVEST-03: Dehaulming & Market Window
- **Condition**: 80-90 days after planting or when foliage begins natural yellowing.
- **Action**: Cut haulms (vines) 10-12 days before digging to cure and harden tuber skin. If cold-storage arrivals cause mandi price depression, cure tubers in aerated heaps under dry paddy straw before deciding on sale vs storage.
