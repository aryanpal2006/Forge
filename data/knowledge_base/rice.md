# ICAR Agronomic Practice Guide: Rice (Oryza sativa)

## RULE-RICE-SUBMERGENCE-01: Flood & Waterlogging Management
- **Condition**: Continuous inundation > 48 hours or rainfall > 65 mm/day during vegetative stage.
- **Action**: Immediately clear tertiary drainage channels and open bund spillways to keep water depth under 10 cm. For flood-prone Kosi/Gangetic floodplains, promote flash-flood submergence tolerant variety **Swarna Sub-1** which can survive 14 days under water.
- **Pest/Disease Risk**: Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB) and Sheath Blight risk spikes when relative humidity > 85% and temperatures range between 28°C–32°C. Apply Streptocycline (1g/10L) + Copper Oxychloride (25g/10L) upon first lesion appearance.

## RULE-RICE-HEAT-02: Anthesis Heat Stress Prevention
- **Condition**: Daytime maximum temperature > 35°C during panicle emergence and flowering.
- **Action**: Irrigate during evening hours to maintain 3–5 cm water layer in the field to buffer canopy temperature via evaporative cooling. Spray 1% Potassium Chloride (KCl) or 2% Urea to alleviate thermal stress and prevent spikelet sterility.

## RULE-RICE-HARVEST-03: Pre-Harvest & Mandi Timing
- **Condition**: 80-85% grains turning golden yellow with moisture content around 20-22%.
- **Action**: Drain field 7-10 days before harvest. Thresh promptly and sun-dry to 14% moisture before taking to APMC mandi. If mandi spot price is above MSP and trending upwards, hold grain in dry hermetic bags for 2-3 weeks.
