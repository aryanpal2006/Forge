# ICAR Agronomic Practice Guide: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum)

## RULE-COTTON-HEAT-01: Square & Boll Shedding Mitigation
- **Condition**: Daytime temperatures exceeding 38°C with dry westerly winds during square initiation or flowering.
- **Action**: Apply foliar spray of 2% DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate) + 1% MOP (Muriate of Potash) or 10 ppm Planofix (NAA) to drastically reduce boll abortion. Provide light furrow irrigation in alternate rows.

## RULE-COTTON-PEST-02: Pink Bollworm & Whitefly Vigilance
- **Condition**: High humidity (>70%) following erratic rains in Deccan or coastal tracts.
- **Action**: Monitor sticky yellow traps for whitefly (>20 adults/leaf) and Delta traps for Pink Bollworm moths. Spray Neem seed kernel extract (NSKE 5%) or Profenofos 50% EC @ 2 ml/L before larvae enter developing green bolls.

## RULE-COTTON-DRAINAGE-03: Heavy Rain & Root Rot Prevention
- **Condition**: Rainfall > 50 mm in 24 hours on deep black soils (Vertisols).
- **Action**: Vertisols have poor internal drainage and shrink-swell properties. Open broad-bed furrows (BBF) to prevent standing water contact with crown roots; drench root zone with Trichoderma viride @ 2.5 kg/ha in farmyard manure.
